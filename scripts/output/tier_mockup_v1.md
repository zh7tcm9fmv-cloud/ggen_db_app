## Method (short)

| Pillar | Max pts | Notes |
|--------|---------|-------|
| ER sortie coverage | 30 | Weighted by stage CP/difficulty; tag-narrow penalty |
| Role stats | ~24 | Percentile ATK/HP/DEF/MOB (SSP LB3) |
| Weapons | ~26 | Range, power, MAP, post-move MAP (master pair table), DEF debuff % |
| Abilities text | ~12 | Heal / DR / EN recovery heuristics |
| Terrain | 8 | Penalize single-environment units |

Two tier labels: **score tier** (absolute) and **meta tier** (top ~8% / 17% / 35% per role among gacha UR EX).
