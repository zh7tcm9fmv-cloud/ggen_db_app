About GGen Eternal Database

GGen Eternal Database (ggendb) is a player-run reference for SD Gundam G Generation ETERNAL. We focus on clear browsing for units, pilots, stages, modifications, and tools such as the damage simulator and team builder, using data aligned with the game where we can verify it.

The site is built and maintained by fans. Stats, formulas, and UI behavior may differ from the live client when the game updates or when interpretation differs—always treat in-game values as the final word for your account.

GGen Eternal Database is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Bandai Namco Entertainment Inc. or its affiliates. Gundam and related marks belong to their respective owners.

If something looks wrong or you have a suggestion, use Contact for how to reach us through the main app.
