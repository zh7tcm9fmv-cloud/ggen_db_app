Contact us

We do not publish a support inbox on this page. To send questions, bug reports, or suggestions, please use the in-app Feedback entry point on the main GGen Database site.

Where to find it

  1. Open GGen Database and go to any browse tab that shows a long list (for example Characters, Units, Supporters, Stages, or Modifications).
  2. Scroll to the bottom of that list, where the pagination row appears.
  3. On the right side of that row, next to “What’s new”, press the Support/Feedback button (contact-style icon). That is the same control as the Feedback shortcut in the pagination footer—it opens our feedback flow in a new tab.

If you are reporting a calculator or data issue, include steps, language, and screenshots when possible so we can reproduce it.

For general information about the project, see About. For data practices, see Privacy Policy.
